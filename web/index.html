🪰 Fruit Fly Arcade

The complete connectome of an adult Drosophila melanogaster — 139,255 neurons, 2.7 million connections, every one of them traced from a real fly by real scientists — simulated live in your browser and pointed at an arcade cabinet. It is not good at this. That is the interesting part, and it is measured rather than asserted.

Fruit Flappy Flylit

A fly in a chamber presses a plate; the plate flaps a bird through pipes. Two modes — the fly as joystick, or the fly as the bird.

Just the flylit

No game. The fly loose on a canvas, with buttons that drive one real population and a live readout of what fired.

Pongdark

The purest fit and the next one built: paddle x = fly x, no buttons, nothing invented. The motor output is the paddle.

Tetrisdark

Ranked the weakest fit for a fly, which is exactly why it is queued: the board fills to a still image while the fly sits, and that is the joke rather than a reason to skip it.

What the measurement says

—

Nothing here is scripted and nothing here learns. This fly cannot improve, because it has no plasticity: it is the same wiring on every run, and a bad score is not a fly having an off day. The honest framing is a benchmark with a control arm, not a leaderboard.
