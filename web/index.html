Fruit Fly — Phase 2 bench: real connectome, real eyes, real body

waking the fly

Move the pointer over the canvas — the fly sees it, with the angular size a flat screen cannot supply, and treats it as a threat. Click on the fly to swat: on the ground it squashes, airborne it is a glancing blow. The buttons drive one real population directly; nothing about the reaction is scripted.

sim below 1.00x is the design working, not a bug. The brain sets the pace and the whole world follows it, so a slow machine shows the fly in honest slow motion with every tuned constant still meaning what it meant.
